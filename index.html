I am a designer with a perspective that bridges the visual, the technical, and the human. My journey began in Industrial Design, where I learned to understand form and function.

My fascination with technology was sparked during a postgraduate program in Digital Animation (Maya), where I discovered the potential of 3D and interactivity. This experience shifted my focus: I realized that design doesn't just live in the interface—it lives in the emotion and the response of the person using it.

To broaden my perspective, I completed a Master’s in Graphic Design at the Nanjing University of the Arts. Studying abroad taught me the value of adaptability and empathy; I learned that every culture and every product requires a unique way of thinking and designing. Today, I bring that global mindset to every project, from initial concept to final implementation.

Throughout my career—working in fintech, education, and cultural sectors—I have focused on designing interfaces and digital resources where accessibility, usability, and performance are the priorities. To further refine this impact, I have specialized in UX/UI Design and Web Development, allowing me to connect design and technology to solve real-world problems through creativity and technical efficiency.

When I'm not focused on professional projects, I'm usually traveling to new places, diving into the energy of electronic music festivals, or heading outdoors for a long walk with my dog. I also have a deep appreciation for discovering international flavors and often spend my downtime revisiting the pixelated worlds of retro video games.
