I am a designer bridging the gap between the visual, the technical, and the human. With a background in Industrial Design, I began my journey focusing on form and function, which later evolved through Digital Animation into a fascination with 3D and interactivity.

My experience earning a Master’s in Graphic Design at Nanjing University of the Arts gave me a global mindset. Studying in China taught me that design requires deep empathy and adaptability—lessons I apply to every project today.

While I have spent more than five years crafting digital products, I’ve always believed that the best designers never stop learning. This curiosity has led me to my current specialization in UX/UI and Frontend Development, where I combine technical efficiency with a user-centered approach to build products that are as functional as they are well-designed.
